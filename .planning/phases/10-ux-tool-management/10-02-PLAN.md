## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-10-04 | Tampering | POST disable/enable/delete endpoints | accept | 单用户本地工具，无需认证。无网络暴露面，前端面板直接触发 |
| T-10-05 | Denial of Service | tools.py endpoints | accept | 工具数量预期 < 100，O(n) 遍历可接受 |
